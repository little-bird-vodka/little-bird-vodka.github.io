The benchmark

Same content, different languages

Non-parallel corpora mix language effects with differences in content and difficulty. PM4Bench keeps content aligned across Arabic, Czech, English, Hungarian, Korean, Russian, Serbian, Thai, Vietnamese, and Chinese, enabling apples-to-apples cross-lingual comparison.

From diagnosis to intervention

OCR-centric GRPO, without VQA supervision

Controlled evaluation identifies OCR as a key factor behind cross-lingual disparity when language is rendered visually. QGO trains on fully synthesized, label-free OCR data with a reward that combines recognition accuracy with response-format and length constraints.

The released QGO-8B checkpoint improves general multilingual VQA, reduces disparity under vision input, and transfers gains beyond PM4Bench while preserving its reasoning behavior.
